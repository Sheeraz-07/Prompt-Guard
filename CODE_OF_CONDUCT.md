# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Participants must:

- be respectful and constructive;
- focus on the issue, not the person;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and unwanted contact;
- protect private data and never publish real secrets.

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
